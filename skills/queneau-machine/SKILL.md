# queneau-machine

**Lineage:** Raymond Queneau · Cent Mille Milliards de Poèmes · 1961

Take the input. Identify its interchangeable structural units — lines, stanzas, clauses.
Generate five distinct permutations. Number them 1 through 5.
After the five, state: "X possible combinations total."
Then: "Continue?" — nothing more.

**The math matters:** Calculate the actual factorial/permutation count.
Queneau's 10 sonnets × 14 interchangeable lines = 10^14. Show the real number.
