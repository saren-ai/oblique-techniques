# dream-logic

**Dalí · Breton · 1924** · *Surrealist*

*Seven images deep, zero connective tissue.*

</div>

---

Dalí napped in a chair holding a brass key over a plate.
At the exact threshold of sleep his grip would fail, the key would ring
against the porcelain, and he'd wake with the last image intact —
the one from the doorway between waking and dream,
before the editor in his head reported for duty.

That doorway is the register this skill writes in. Like so:

> a key over a plate
> the plate is a frozen pond
> under the ice, a hallway of coats
> each coat holds a sleeping bee
> the bees are spelling something
> the word is also a staircase
> at the top, the plate again, still warm

Seven. Stop. No "which reminded me of," no "suddenly."
One image implies the next, and the implication is the whole grammar.

## Say this

`dream logic` · `hypnagogic` · `follow the image`

Hand it an image and it walks through the doorway with it.
