# Catalog

A human-readable index of every stratagem in **Oblique Technique**.
Thirteen built or scaffolded. The structure is designed to grow toward one hundred.

`✓` = written · `◦` = scaffolded, content pending

---

## Surrealist

- **cut-up** `✓` — *Burroughs · Gysin · 1959.* Fragment the input at irregular intervals and recombine by adjacency alone. The rearrangement is the meaning.
- **exquisite-corpse** `✓` — *Breton · Ernst · 1925.* The fold, inverted: the human plays blind while the machine keeps the body. Each line answers only the line above it; the whole is unfolded at the reveal.
- **automatic-dispatch** `✓` — *Breton · Éluard · 1919.* Take the prompt as a first image and follow it with no transitions, no logic, no apology.
- **dream-logic** `✓` — *Dalí · Breton · 1924.* Follow an image by its internal hypnagogic logic for seven steps. No connective tissue.

## Oulipo

- **n+7** `✓` — *Mathews · Oulipo · 1961.* Replace every noun with the seventh noun following it in the dictionary.
- **lipogram** `✓` — *Perec · La Disparition · 1969.* A named letter is exiled completely; write around the gap without announcing it.
- **queneau-machine** `✓` — *Queneau · 1961.* Identify interchangeable units, generate permutations, and state the true combinatorial count.

## Fluxus

- **event-score** `✓` — *Maciunas · Ono · 1962.* One to four lines of instruction — impossible, quiet, or devastating. The instruction is the complete work.
- **oblique-interrupt** `✓` — *Eno · Schmidt · 1975.* For the stuck: one lateral constraint that is not advice and turns out to be correct.

## Situationist

- **détournement** `✓` — *Debord · Wolman · 1956.* Reverse a text's allegiance using only its own vocabulary. Never announce the reversal.
