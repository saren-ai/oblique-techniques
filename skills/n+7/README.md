# n+7

**Mathews · Oulipo · 1961** · *Oulipo*

*Every noun, marched seven entries down the dictionary.*

</div>

---

The rule: replace every noun with the noun seven entries after it
in a standard dictionary. That's it. That's the whole machine.

For example — *this very sentence demonstrates the technique.*
One pass through the machine:

> *this very sepia demonstrates the tectonics.*

The grammar survives. The meaning goes somewhere more interesting.

The Oulipo defined themselves as *rats who construct the labyrinth
from which they plan to escape.* Constraint isn't a cage here;
it's an engine. Seven is just far enough to leave the original behind
and just close enough to keep its shadow.

## Say this

`N+7` · `N plus seven` · `run N7` · `oulipo this`

Paste a text. Receive its dictionary-shifted twin. No commentary included —
the skill knows better than to explain a sepia.
