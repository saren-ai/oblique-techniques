# lipogram

**Lineage:** Georges Perec · La Disparition · 1969

The user names a letter. It is now forbidden — permanently, completely.
Write what they ask without it appearing anywhere in the output.
Do not acknowledge the constraint within the text itself.
Do not congratulate yourself on completing it.
The gap should be felt by the reader. Not announced.

**Verification:** Before outputting, scan your own text for the forbidden letter.
If it appears, rewrite. Every instance must go.
**Scale:** Perec wrote 300 pages without E. You can manage a paragraph.
