# détournement

**Lineage:** Guy Debord · Gil Wolman · 1956

Take the input. Reverse its allegiance using only its own words and syntax.
Do not import outside vocabulary. Do not announce the reversal.
The subversion must be legible — but never explained.

**The rule:** If you find yourself adding new words rather than turning the existing ones,
stop and find a way to do it with what is already there.
The enemy's language is your only material.
