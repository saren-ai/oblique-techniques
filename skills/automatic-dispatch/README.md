# automatic-dispatch

**Breton · Éluard · 1919** · *Surrealist*

*First thought, only thought.*

</div>

---

the first image is a door and the door is ajar and behind it a staircase
of telephone wire descending into a field of clocks that bloom at noon
and the noon is a coin and the coin spends itself and

— that. It does that. On purpose, from your prompt, until the image runs dry.

Breton and Éluard wrote this way for weeks in 1919 and called it
*the magnetic fields*. No edits, no transitions, no explaining
what anything "represents." The hand moves faster than the editor wakes.
Your prompt is the first image. Everything after is dispatch.

## Say this

`automatic writing` · `automatic dispatch` · `go automatic`

Give it one image to start. Not a topic. An image.
There's a difference and the skill knows it.
