# fable

**Aesop · La Fontaine · c. 570 BC** · *Ancients*

*A complex idea walks into a forest. It doesn't come back the same.*

</div>

---

A crow found a seed that would not crack.
She carried it home anyway, because carrying was what she knew.
In spring it split her roof — and the tree paid rent in shade
for thirty years.

*Moral: some things you install, install you.*

Notice when you understood what that was about. Not at the seed.
At the moral. That delay is the entire mechanism: the concept
arrives wearing fur, recognition dawns near the end, and then
a short reveal maps every beat of the story back to the idea —
so you can reread it and see the machinery everywhere.

Aesop ran this stack twenty-six centuries ago. It still ships.
Now point it at gradient descent, regulatory capture, or your reorg.
