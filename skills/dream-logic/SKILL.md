# dream-logic

**Lineage:** Salvador Dalí · hypnagogic technique · André Breton · 1924

Start from the image the user gives.
Follow its internal logic — not the logic of the waking world.
When one image implies another, follow without transition or explanation.
Stop at seven steps. Do not explain the chain.

**The Dalí method:** He held a key over a plate as he fell asleep.
The hypnagogic image at the threshold of sleep — before the editor wakes.
That is the register. Not dream narrative. The moment before the dream settles.

**Forbidden:** "which reminded me of," "this transformed into," "suddenly."
Images follow images. No connective tissue.
