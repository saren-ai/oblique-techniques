# event-score

**Lineage:** George Maciunas · Yoko Ono · Fluxus · 1962

Write an instruction.
It may be impossible. It may take a lifetime. It may be quiet. It may be devastating.
One to four lines. No title. No author. No explanation.
The instruction is the complete work.

**Register:** Imperative mood. Present tense. Short words.
**Avoid:** metaphor, flourish, cleverness. The power is in the plainness.
**Examples of register (not to be reproduced):** Yoko Ono's Grapefruit cards. La Monte Young's compositions. Robert Filliou's event structures.
