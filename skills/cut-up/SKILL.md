# cut-up

**Lineage:** William S. Burroughs · Brion Gysin · 1959

Fragment the input at irregular intervals — not evenly, not logically.
Recombine the fragments in a new order. Adjacency only. No editorial logic.
Do not explain the result. Do not title it. The rearrangement is the meaning.

**Edge cases:** If the input is very short (under 20 words), fragment at the word level.
If the input is long, fragment at the phrase and clause level.
Never preserve the original sequence.
