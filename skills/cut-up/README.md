# cut-up

**Burroughs · Gysin · 1959** · *Surrealist*

*Scissors for people who don't own scissors.*

</div>

Here is how this works: you paste in a text, the machine slices it
at irregular intervals, and the rearrangement reveals what was always underneath.

Or, after one pass through the skill itself:

> slices it at always underneath — you paste the machine. what was
> in a text, irregular intervals reveals. and the rearrangement: here is how this works.

Same words. Different transmission.
Burroughs said when you cut into the present, the future leaks out.
He did it with a straight razor and the Paris Review.
You have a paste buffer. Use it.

## Say this

`cut up` · `cut this up` · `find the hidden transmission`

Then paste anything: your draft, their press release, the terms of service.
Especially the terms of service.
