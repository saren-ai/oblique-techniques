# entendre-engine

**Duchamp · Rrose Sélavy · 1922** · *Dada*

*Finds out what your text has been saying behind your back.*

</div>

---

In 1920 Marcel Duchamp invented a second self, dressed her in furs,
and signed her name to his work: **Rrose Sélavy.**
Say it aloud. *Eros, c'est la vie.*
The pun was in the pronunciation the whole time, wearing a hat.

That's the engine. It doesn't add double meanings to your text —
it surfaces the ones already in the room, standing quietly by the bar,
waiting to be acknowledged. It's not what your text says.
It's what your text has been saying on the side.

Two house rules, strictly kept:
nothing imported (if the double isn't already there, it stays out),
and nothing explained (an explained entendre is a single entendre).

Run it on your meeting notes.
Find out who's really *circling back.*

## Say this

`double entendre` · `double meaning` · `find the innuendo` · `what else is this saying`

Paste anything that was written carefully. Carefully is where they hide.
